Concept art of the play screen of an original PC rhythm game, 16:9, seen as the player sees it. A note highway in perspective runs from a vanishing point near the top centre down to the bottom of the screen, divided into eight lanes: one wider turntable lane at the far left and seven key lanes. Rectangular notes slide down the lanes toward a bright horizontal judgment line near the bottom. Show: about ten short tap notes at different distances in two alternating colours plus one accent colour in the centre lane; one long hold note with a distinct head cap, a dimmer body and a differently shaped tail cap, currently being held so its body glows where it meets the line; one wide translucent band (a 'side note') lying flat under the left half of the highway, clearly beneath the key notes; one differently shaped turntable note in the far-left lane; a hit effect bursting on one lane exactly at the judgment line; a soft light beam rising from one pressed lane behind the notes. Below the judgment line sits the control deck of the 'gear' (the frame of the highway) and two side rails run along the highway's edges. The floor of the highway is dark and plain so the notes are the brightest things on screen. Background outside the highway: near-black. Style: cut optical glass and light. Notes are thick bevelled bars of clear crystal glass with a saturated inner colour (ice white, cobalt blue, amber accent) and a razor-bright leading edge on the side that touches the judgment line. The long note body is a hollow glass tube with a thin thread of light inside that brightens and flows when held; its head is a full glass bar, its tail a slimmer wedge-cut cap. The side note is a sheet of frosted glass with two luminous edge lines in violet. The turntable note is a faceted ruby-red hexagonal prism. The hit effect is a short burst of spectral light: a white flash, then thin rainbow-dispersion shards fanning sideways along the judgment line, gone in six frames. The gear is satin black anodised aluminium with chamfered edges; the side rails are clear glass light-guides carrying a thin line of white light; the control deck is a slab of smoked glass over machined metal with a small round lens in the middle. Judgment line: a thin white-hot line with a faint spectral fringe. Mood: precise, elegant, laboratory optics. Original design: it must not resemble any existing game.
